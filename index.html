Why this exists

So studying never depends on who you know.

Not everyone reaches college with a folder of notes and a senior ready to help. Many of us turned up in a new city with little more than a suitcase and a timetable.

So we gathered what we could find — notes, question papers, syllabi — and arranged it here, once, for everyone. Open the page, take the file you need, and get back to studying. No favour, no condition, no waiting.

Exam tips

How toppers actually use this

Short, practical habits that turn a pile of PDFs into marks.

1

Start with the PYQs

Solve the last three years first. You will see which units repeat every time, and where the marks really are.

2

Go unit by unit

Finish one unit completely before starting the next. Half-finished units are where marks quietly disappear.

3

Make one formula sheet

Write every formula of the subject on a single page. Revise that page daily in the last week.

4

Mark the repeats

A question that appears every year is free marks. Highlight those in your notes and never skip them.

5

Practise in real time

Attempt one full paper in three hours, no breaks, no notes. Speed is half the battle.

6

Revise, don't re-read

Close the book and write what you remember. That is what actually sticks in the exam hall.

FAQ

Questions students ask

Is everything really free?

Yes. No login, no fee, and no locked section. Every note, paper and book opens without paying anything.

Do I need to create an account?

No account and no sign-up. Open the site and start studying straight away.

How do I download notes?

Pick your department, tap a subject, then use the download buttons. Files open in a new tab.

Which departments are covered?

Ten: First Year (Common), Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, CSE, ECE, Electronics & Instrumentation, Biotechnology, Food Technology and Biomedical.

How can I contribute notes or books?

Use the Share button and send the file link. It gets added for everyone who comes after you.

Is my activity tracked?

No. There are no ads, no tracking and nothing to log in to.
